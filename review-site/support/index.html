Gmail 登录帮助Gmail sign-in help

1

选择 GmailChoose Gmail

在 Mine Mail 的设置 → 账户 → 添加账户中选择 Gmail。In Mine Mail, open Settings → Accounts → Add Account and choose Gmail.

2

在系统浏览器授权Authorize in your system browser

确认 Google 页面显示的应用名称为 Mine Mail，选择正确账户，并阅读请求的权限。Mine Mail 不会要求你在应用界面输入 Google 密码。Confirm the Google page names Mine Mail, choose the correct account, and review the requested access. Mine Mail does not ask for your Google password inside the app.

3

返回 Mine MailReturn to Mine Mail

Google 会将结果返回本机的随机回环地址；浏览器成功页出现后可回到应用等待首次同步。Google returns the result to a random loopback address on your device. After the browser confirms success, return to the app for initial sync.

仍然看到“仅限测试用户”？ 这表示当前 Google OAuth 项目仍处于 Testing，或你安装的是审核前构建。公开审核通过并切换到 Production 后，普通 Google 用户才可授权。Still seeing “test users only”? The Google OAuth project is still in Testing, or you installed a pre-review build. General Google users can authorize only after production publication and any required review.

本地数据在哪里？Where is local data?

Windows 应用数据与日志位于：On Windows, app data and logs are under:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\com.minemail.desktop

授权 token 与邮箱授权密码另存于操作系统凭据存储。OAuth tokens and mail authorization secrets are stored separately in the OS credential store.

远程图片与隐私Remote images and privacy

远程图片默认自动加载。图片主机可能获知你的 IP 地址和大致打开时间。可在设置中改为“询问”或“阻止”。Remote images load automatically by default. Their hosts may learn your IP address and approximate open time. Change the setting to Ask or Blocked if preferred.
